# FieldQuest - Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**Tagline:** *Your neighbourhood is more interesting than your feed.*

FieldQuest is an open-source, AI-powered, offline-first nature exploration Progressive Web App (PWA). It inspires urban, suburban, and rural residents to put down their screens and engage directly with their local living environment. Through structured, micro-duration observational "missions" generated by a resilient **3-Tier AI Engine** (Production Cloud AI via Google Gemini / Groq Cloud, local open-weight AI via Ollama with `qwen2.5:3b` / `qwen3:4b`, or an instant deterministic offline fallback), users observe biodiversity, patterns, sounds, and plant adaptations right on their street, park, balcony, or walking trail.

FieldQuest stands in direct opposition to endless scrolling, digital fatigue, and generic chatbot wrappers. The application prioritizes:
1. **Real-world exploration over screen time** (missions are screen-down friendly).
2. **True local-first privacy** (no accounts, no cloud surveillance, no continuous GPS).
3. **Zero-Configuration Production AI with 3-Tier Failover** (free-tier cloud models run out-of-the-box on the server; if quotas expire or offline, requests auto-failover to local Ollama or curated field missions with zero downtime).
4. **Open-weight AI sovereignty** (optional local inference for developers and privacy purists).

## 2. Problem Statement & Target Audience

### 2.1 The Problem
- **Nature Deficit & Screen Addiction:** Adults and youth spend an average of 7+ hours per day staring at screens. Even when walking outside, people remain glued to notification feeds.
- **Ecological Blindness:** Most people pass by dozens of distinct plant, bird, insect, and fungus species daily without noticing biological adaptations, seasonal shifts, or micro-habitats.
- **Intrusive Apps:** Existing outdoor apps often demand mandatory accounts, continuous invasive GPS tracking, subscription paywalls, or cloud uploads, creating high friction and battery drain.
- **Generic AI Chatbots:** Existing AI apps are often generic chat screens that increase screen time rather than guiding real-world physical action.
- **AI Infrastructure Barriers:** Requiring end users to download 4GB+ LLM models or manage complex API keys deters casual nature lovers.

### 2.2 Target Personas
1. **The Overstimulated Digital Worker (Priya, 28):** Works remotely, suffers from screen fatigue, has a 20-minute afternoon break. Needs a quick, grounding sensory exercise in her neighbourhood park without signing up for yet another SaaS tool or configuring developer keys.
2. **The Urban Balcony Naturalist (Tanvir, 34):** Lives in an apartment with a small balcony or urban street. Wonders what biodiversity exists in concrete landscapes and wants micro-observations adapted to urban environments.
3. **The Curious Family / Educator (Elena, 41):** Wants to take children on a 30-minute weekend exploration in a botanical garden or local woodland, focusing on tactile learning (comparing leaf venation, bird calls) without competitive gamification or screens.
4. **The Privacy-Conscious Outdoor Adventurer (Kai, 31):** Explores remote trails with spotty cellular coverage. Demands complete offline capability and sovereign data ownership.

## 3. Product Goals & Success Metrics

### 3.1 Primary Goals
- Provide structured, bite-sized nature observational missions tailored to user time (15, 30, 60 mins), environment, interests, and language.
- Ensure the app functions completely offline after the initial load, saving all adventures and journal entries inside local browser storage (IndexedDB).
- Deliver **Cloud Naturalist Engine (Production AI)** powered by free-tier production cloud models (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash / Groq Cloud Llama 3.3) managed securely on the server with zero client key configuration.
- Implement an **automatic 3-tier failover pipeline**: Production Cloud AI -> Local AI (Ollama) -> Curated Offline Library, ensuring missions never fail.
- Preserve full support for sovereign local open-weight AI (Ollama with `qwen2.5:3b`, `qwen3:4b`, or Google Gemma 2) for self-hosted instances.
- Provide a clear, instant Curated Field Library mode that requires zero external AI infrastructure or network connectivity.

### 3.2 Key Metrics (Local & Self-Reported)
- **Completion Rate:** Percentage of started adventures that reach completion or reflection.
- **Outdoor Observation Time:** Cumulative self-reported time spent outdoors vs. screen interaction time.
- **Local Persistence Reliability:** Zero data loss for local journal entries across restarts.
- **Time to First Mission:** Under 5 seconds from opening the app to starting an adventure in demo mode, under 15 seconds with local LLM generation.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### 5.1 Landing & Onboarding
- Editorial "Forest Atelier" landing page introducing FieldQuest's ethos.
- Featured active mission showcase.
- Direct CTA into Adventure Setup.
- Clear explanation of open-weight AI, offline local storage, and privacy guarantees.

### 5.2 Adventure Setup
- **Duration Options:** 15 minutes (Quick Sensory Walk), 30 minutes (Standard Field Observation), 60 minutes (Deep Ecological Expedition).
- **Environments:** Urban Neighbourhood, City Park, Balcony / Porch, Garden, Forest / Woodland Trail.
- **Interests (Multi-select):** Plants & Leaves, Birds & Calls, Insects & Pollinators, Trees & Bark, Sounds & Wind, Patterns & Textures, Micro-Biodiversity.
- **Difficulty:** Easy (Accessible, sensory), Exploratory (Comparative, analytical).
- **Language:** English (`en`).
- **Accessibility:** Mobility-friendly / sit-down observation option.

### 5.3 Multi-Tier AI Mission Generation Engine
- **Modular backend architecture** orchestrating mission generation across three distinct tiers:
  1. **Tier 1 — Cloud Naturalist Engine (Production AI):** Powered by Google Gemini (1.5 Flash / 2.0 Flash) or Groq Cloud (Llama 3.3 70B / Qwen 2.5) free tiers. Enabled via server-side `.env` keys (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, `GROQ_API_KEY`). Zero configuration required from client explorers.
  2. **Tier 2 — Local Open-Weight AI (Ollama):** Optional self-hosted inference via local Ollama instances running `qwen2.5:3b`, `qwen3:4b`, or Google Gemma 2. Kept completely private on `:11434`.
  3. **Tier 3 — Curated Field Library (100% Offline Fallback):** High-fidelity deterministic naturalist missions mapped to requested habitats, interests, and duration.
- **Automatic 3-Tier Failover:** If Tier 1 hits cloud rate limits (429), quota exhaustion, or network disconnects, the engine seamlessly fails over to Tier 2 (Ollama) if available, and finally to Tier 3 (Curated Field Library). Missions **never** fail or throw blocking error popups.
- **Strict schema validation** via Zod ensuring each mission contains:
  - `id`: Unique stable string
  - `title`: Concise descriptive mission title
  - `objective`: Primary sensory goal
  - `instructions`: Step-by-step guidance
  - `estimatedMinutes`: Number
  - `observationCriteria`: Specific items/patterns to seek
  - `completionCriteria`: Observable condition to mark complete
  - `safetyGuidance`: Strict safety directives (never consume unknown plants, avoid touching wildlife)
  - `reflectionQuestion`: Contemplative post-mission question

### 5.4 Active Adventure Flow
- **Step-by-step mission progression:** One active mission at a time.
- **Session Controls:** Complete mission, Skip mission, Pause timer, Resume, Abandon adventure.
- **Screen-Down Mode:** Screen dims to a soothing low-power dark display prompting user to put the device in their pocket until they hear or feel a reminder chime.
- **Observation Capture:** Note field observations, capture or select a local photo, and rate findings.
- **Adventure Summary:** Displays completion percentage, total outdoor minutes, and encourages reflection.

### 5.5 Local Nature Journal & History (`/journal`)
- Built on Dexie (IndexedDB).
- Clean two-tab architecture: **Adventures History** and **Field Notes & Observations**.
- Observation notes format with structured naturalist formatting (bracket headers, markdown subheadings, anatomical bullet points).
- Interactive **Botanical Reference Plate Inline Drawer** expanding seamlessly inside the observation card without modals or redirects.
- Complete data portability: JSON export and file upload backup restoration with transactional safety.
- Operational completely offline with zero network connectivity required.

### 5.6 Creative Field Studio (`/studio`)
- Dedicated botanical art workshop and morphological study suite.
- **Specimen Art Director:** Preset biological organisms (*Bilateral Oak Venation*, *Sycamore Bark Lichen*, *Pine Cone Fibonacci Spiral*, etc.) and custom subject inputs.
- **Historical Art Styles:** 4 selectable presets (*Vintage 18th-century Lithograph*, *Microscopic Anatomy*, *Pressed Herbarium Specimen*, *Field Graphite Pencil*).
- **Procedural Studio Artboard:** Live rendering canvas, random seed generator, high-resolution PNG download, and direct "Save to Journal" integration.
- **Herbarium Archive & Folio Library:** 7 curated classical reference plates (Venation, Bark, Margins, Conifers, Ferns, Lichens, Seeds) with anatomical diagnostic keys and "Use as Studio Inspiration" controls.
- **Archived Specimen Gallery:** Instant browsing of personal illustrations stored in IndexedDB.

### 5.7 Philosophy & Science Foundation Modal
- Accessible directly from footer and landing page.
- 5 comprehensive educational chapters: Attention Restoration Theory (ART), Botanical Functional Morphology & Bioindicators, Acoustic Ecology & Soundscape Stratification, Urban Ecology & Micro-Habitats, and Sovereign Privacy & Offline Resilience.

### 5.8 Settings & Diagnostics
- AI Engine status check (`/api/v1/ai/status`).
- Streamlined configuration UI:
  - **Cloud Naturalist Engine Card:** Displays active production status (`Ready Out-of-the-Box`, `Auto-Failover Protection`), server-managed free-tier credentials, zero client input required.
  - **Local AI Card (Ollama / Podman):** For self-hosters and developers wanting local sovereign open-weight inference on port 11434.
  - **Failover Chain Indicator:** Live visual trace of the 3-step pipeline (`Production Cloud AI` -> `Local AI (Ollama)` -> `Curated Field Library`).
- Storage stats (number of adventures, storage used in IndexedDB).
- Complete data wipe option for privacy.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

### 6.1 Performance
- Initial bundle load < 300KB gzipped.
- Offline cached app shell loads in < 200ms.
- Dexie read/write transactions execute in < 20ms.

### 6.2 Offline-First (PWA)
- Service Worker precaches all essential assets (HTML, CSS, JS, fonts, offline SVGs).
- IndexedDB holds user data locally. No network required for existing adventures or journal entries.

### 6.3 Security & Privacy
- **Zero Remote Storage:** User notes, journal reflections, and photos remain on device.
- **Zero Invasive Permissions:** No forced geolocation, no biometric tracking, no camera permission forced (standard file input for photos).
- **Safe Content Generation:** Strict AI safety prompts prohibiting toxic foraging, trespassing, or hazardous physical activity.
- **Client Security:** API keys are never exposed or handled on client devices; all production keys remain server-side in `.env`.

### 6.4 Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all color contrasts.
- Keyboard navigable controls and visible focus indicators.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## 7. Explicit Non-Goals (MVP Out of Scope)
- ❌ No user accounts, passwords, or authentication servers.
- ❌ No cloud database (no PostgreSQL, MySQL, or MongoDB in MVP).
- ❌ No Redis caching layer (unnecessary for single-instance / local MVP).
- ❌ No continuous background GPS path recording or step counters.
- ❌ No social feed, public likes, or competitive leaderboards.
- ❌ No remote image hosting CDN.

## 8. Assumptions, Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud AI quota exhausted or network unavailable | High | Automatic 3-tier failover: falls back silently to local Ollama if active, or instantly to Curated Field Library with zero user interruption. |
| User does not have Ollama installed or GPU is slow | High | Cloud Naturalist Engine runs by default with free-tier server keys, backed by Curated Field Library. |
| LLM produces hallucinated or malformed JSON | High | Strict Zod parsing on the backend with automated retry/fallback, and defensive validation in the frontend. |
| User wanders into dangerous situations | Critical | Every mission schema enforces explicit `safetyGuidance`. Prompts instruct never to ingest wild plants, trespass, or disturb wildlife. |
| Browser IndexedDB eviction by OS | Medium | Provide prominent "Export Journal (JSON)" utility and warn users about browser data clearing policies. |
